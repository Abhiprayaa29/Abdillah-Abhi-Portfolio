04 / Identity

About Me

“I enjoy exploring the relationship between design and technology. I care about how digital products communicate visually, how people interact with them, and how the systems behind them are engineered.”

I am an Informatics Engineering student at Universitas Pembangunan Nasional “Veteran” Yogyakarta. Rather than treating design and software engineering as separate disciplines, I cultivate both in parallel. Understanding user interfaces allows me to architect software that feels purposeful; understanding distributed architectures and cryptography allows me to design interfaces grounded in technical reality.

01 As a Designer

I design complete design systems, user flows, and high-density enterprise platforms. My focus is typography, spatial hierarchy, accessibility, and interactive prototypes in Figma, complemented by 3D asset modeling in Blender.

02 As a Coder

I build resilient full-stack web applications, asynchronous APIs in FastAPI and Node.js, real-time WebSocket state engines, and hardware-backed cryptographic authentication platforms with verified test suites.
